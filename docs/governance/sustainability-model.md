# Sustainability and services

**Starting framework adopted on 8 November 2026.** The model will evolve with actual costs, available capacity and practical experience. Changes will be explained publicly and agreed service terms will be respected.

See [Support OMI](/support/) for the suggested HUF amounts, service planning examples and linked support channel. Reference amounts do not create payment-provider tiers or an immediately purchasable service.

## What stays free

Open Manuscript Studio remains free and open source. Writing, opening and saving manuscripts, core import and export, open formats and self-hosting remain free. Contributions are optional; they do not change manuscript handling or access to security and data-loss fixes.

Costly shared storage, machine translation, OCR and similar services may have transparent capacity limits. Such limits must preserve downloading and exporting one's own manuscripts. A paid service or contribution does not create exclusive ownership of the open specifications.

## Four complementary sources

| Source | Purpose | Commitment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Individual contributions | Voluntary community support | Common maintenance, testing and development |
| Annual institutional contributions | A predictable maintenance base | Transparent use of contributions and public progress reporting |
| Separately commissioned services | Payment for agreed work and infrastructure | A written scope for deployment, OJS/OMP connection, training or care |
| Grants and funded development | Larger improvements | A defined work plan, budget and deliverables |

Start with three suggested monthly individual levels, optional one-time support and one annual institutional supporter framework. The higher institutional levels shown on the support page are for later expansion. An annual contribution is not a per-user licence or a bundle of support hours.

## Services and capacity

The service examples use an internal planning rate, estimated hours and assumed infrastructure costs. They are **planning amounts excluding VAT**, not final consumer prices. A written quote must state the provider, applicable VAT or exemption, final amount, exact scope, exclusions and delivery arrangements.

The monthly care example combines two hours of maintenance and assistance with assumed infrastructure and backup costs. Extra work needs prior agreement. Hosting, paid APIs, licences, migration and custom development are assessed separately. Institution-owned hosting and OMI-managed hosting must have clearly allocated responsibilities.

The initial pilot is limited to two institutions. Expansion depends on actual workload and service readiness. Contracted care requires verified deployment, updates, preservation of documents, backups and restoration of a compatible application and data state. Agreements must cover data handling, termination and data return. Unlimited storage, unlimited support, 24-hour cover and automatic availability guarantees are not part of the starting model.

## Payments

The support page links to the project's existing GitHub Sponsors destination. Available tiers, recipient, currency, recurrence and cancellation must be checked at that destination before confirming a payment. GitHub tiers are in USD; the suggested HUF figures are reference amounts, not a guaranteed currency conversion. This website does not collect card details or start payments.

Before activating further payment methods, identify the recipient and provider, clarify accounting and applicable tax treatment, and test payment, cancellation and refund handling. Institutional contributions and commissioned services must have distinct terms and appropriate records. The word “support” alone does not establish a tax exemption.

## Transparency and independence

Publish a short quarterly summary of income, operating expenses and completed work. Supporter names and logos appear only with consent. Contributions do not buy editorial, peer-review or scholarly influence, exclusive roadmap control or earlier access to security fixes.

Measure actual recurring income, direct costs, maintenance time and client work separately. Include the value of maintenance work when assessing sustainability, and avoid counting client infrastructure in both the common budget and a service quote. Grant income follows its own permitted purpose and funding period.

## First 90 days

| Phase | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Days 1–15 | Confirm actual costs, recipient, accounting, payment eligibility and available service time |
| Days 16–30 | Complete public terms, contribution options and payment/cancellation checks |
| Days 31–60 | Run up to two scoped institutional pilots and record actual effort |
| Days 61–90 | Prepare the first public summary and review amounts, capacity and service scope |

The first review follows this rollout rather than an assumed calendar launch. Later reviews use practical evidence. Changes to public reference amounts must not silently alter existing service agreements.
